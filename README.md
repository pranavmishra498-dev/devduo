# DevDuo 🚀

### Technology Solutions for Students, Startups & Small Businesses

DevDuo is a technology startup focused on building practical, affordable, and customized digital solutions.

We help students, startups, colleges, and small businesses turn their ideas into working technology products.

## 💡 What We Do

### 💻 Web Development
- Business websites
- Portfolio websites
- Landing pages
- College/project websites
- Custom web solutions

### 📊 Data Analytics
- Data cleaning and analysis
- SQL-based data solutions
- Power BI dashboards
- Business reports and insights
- KPI and performance dashboards

### 🤖 AI & Automation
- AI-powered applications
- LLM-based solutions
- AI chatbots
- Prompt-based workflows
- Email and business process automation

### 🎓 Student & College Solutions
- Academic project development
- Project guidance and implementation
- Technical documentation
- Demo-ready applications
- Custom solutions for college requirements

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make useful technology accessible to students, startups, and small businesses by providing practical solutions at affordable prices.
